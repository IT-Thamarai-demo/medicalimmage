## ❓ 2. Problem Statement
**The Challenge:**
In many healthcare settings, there's a growing gap between the number of X-rays taken and the availability of experts to read them. This leads to:
*   **Wait times:** Patients waiting days for results.
*   **Fatigue:** Doctors analyzing hundreds of scans daily, which can lead to oversight.
*   **Access:** Many remote clinics don't have a specialist on-site 24/7.

---

## 💡 3. Solution Overview
**The "Anti-Gravity" Answer:**
Our system uses advanced **Pattern Recognition AI** to instantly scan a chest X-ray and identify signs of pneumonia. It doesn't just give a "Yes" or "No"—it acts as a **Visual Guide**, highlighting exactly where it sees suspicious patterns. It's like a high-tech highlighter for a doctor’s eyes.

---

## ⚙️ 4. How the System Works (Step-by-Step)
**Analogy:** *It works like a smart photo filter that can spot health issues.*

1.  **Upload:** The doctor simply uploads the digital X-ray image to our dashboard.
2.  **Analysis:** In less than 3 seconds, the AI "looks" at thousands of tiny patterns in the lungs.
3.  **Visualization:** It creates a **Heatmap**—a thermal-style overlay that turns red in the areas where pneumonia is detected.
4.  **Reporting:** It automatically generates a professional PDF report containing the findings, the visual evidence, and a confidence score.
5.  **Review:** The doctor reviews the report and the highlighted X-ray to make the final diagnosis.
